## NuFast-LBL (Denton & Parke) — `jaxnu/nufast.py`

`jaxnu/nufast.py` is a close, line-by-line JAX transcription of the reference
C++ implementation `NuFast_LBL.cpp` released with

> P. B. Denton and S. J. Parke, *Fast and Accurate Algorithm for Calculating
> Long-Baseline Neutrino Oscillation Probabilities with Matter Effects:
> NuFast*, Phys. Rev. D **110**, 073005 (2024), arXiv:2405.02400.
> https://github.com/PeterDenton/NuFast-LBL

It is a port, not an independent re-derivation from the published formulas: the
intermediate variable naming tracks the original source closely. NuFast-LBL is
distributed under the MIT License, reproduced verbatim below. Users of the
`nufast` backend should cite Denton & Parke (2024), as the original authors
request.

## PREM — `jaxnu/earth.py`

The Preliminary Reference Earth Model density polynomials are taken from the
published tables of

> A. M. Dziewonski and D. L. Anderson, *Preliminary reference Earth model*,
> Phys. Earth Planet. Inter. **25**, 297 (1981).

These are published numerical coefficients, reimplemented here; no third-party
code is incorporated.

## BS05 solar model — `examples/data/bs05_agsop.dat`

Tabulated standard-solar-model electron-density profile from

> J. N. Bahcall, A. M. Serenelli and S. Basu, *New solar opacities, abundances,
> helioseismology, and neutrino fluxes*, Astrophys. J. **621**, L85 (2005),
> astro-ph/0412440.
> http://www.sns.ias.edu/~jnb/SNdata/

The BS05 tables are distributed publicly by the authors for community use. The
file is redistributed here unmodified for convenience and for reproducibility
of the solar examples. If you use it, cite Bahcall, Serenelli & Basu (2005).

**Note:** the original distribution carries no explicit machine-readable
licence. It is redistributed here in reliance on its public release for
scientific use; if the authors or their estate object, it will be replaced by a
download step.

## nu-waves — `examples/nuwaves/`

Reference oscillogram configurations used to cross-check jaxnu's atmospheric
plots against

> A. Blanchet, *nu-waves*, https://github.com/nadrino/nu-waves

The figures in `examples/nuwaves/` are generated by jaxnu itself and reproduce
the reference plots of that library; no nu-waves code is incorporated.
